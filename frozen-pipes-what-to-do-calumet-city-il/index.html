Frozen Pipes in Calumet City, IL: What to Do and What Not To

A frozen pipe is a warning. The damage comes when it splits, and that often happens after the thaw. Here is what to do in the first hour.

If a pipe is frozen right now

If a faucet gives only a trickle or nothing in freezing weather, a pipe is probably frozen. Open that faucet, locate the frozen section if you can and warm it slowly with a hair dryer or warm towels. Start near the faucet and work back toward the freeze. Never use an open flame, a torch or a space heater placed against the pipe.

If you see a bulge, a crack or water, shut off the main valve and call. Our emergency line is open 24/7. For what a plumber does once a pipe has split, see our burst pipe repair page.

What to do, in order

  • ✓Open the affected faucet so melting water has somewhere to go
  • ✓Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer, heating pad or warm towels
  • ✓Check other faucets and the ceiling and walls near the pipe for leaks
  • ✓If the pipe has split, close the main valve and open the lowest faucet
  • ✓Call a plumber if you cannot reach the frozen section or see damage

Where pipes freeze first

Exterior walls

Pipes in walls that face the wind lose heat quickly, especially when insulation is thin.

Garages and unheated rooms

A garage with a utility sink or a laundry line can drop below freezing overnight.

Crawl spaces and rim joists

Cold air leaks around the sill plate and hits pipes close to the outside.

Outdoor hose bibs

A hose left attached traps water. Remove hoses and close the interior shutoff in fall.

If you leave for several days in winter, know where your main shutoff is and consider having a neighbor check the house. A boiler failure while you are away is a common cause of a freeze, and our boiler repair page explains what to check.

Why the leak shows up after the thaw

Water expands as it freezes, and the ice plug does the damage by building pressure between itself and a closed faucet. The pipe may split at that point or nearby, but ice can hold the split closed. When the ice melts, water pours through the crack. That is why a pipe can look fine during the freeze and flood the ceiling a few hours later.

After a hard freeze, check the water meter with every fixture off. If the dial moves, there is a leak. Look at ceilings and walls near the suspected pipe for dampness.

Calumet City local note: a cold lake supply and your main shutoff

The Calumet City Water Department's 2024 report says the city draws all of its water from Lake Michigan, with 80 percent supplied by Hammond and 20 percent by Chicago. Water from a lake source arrives cold in winter, which is one reason supply lines near an exterior wall are the usual place for a freeze.

Find your main shutoff before the first hard freeze and test that it turns. If it will not close, a plumber can replace it, which is easier in October than at 2 a.m. in January. For leaks near the street or the meter, the city water department handles the main, and our water service line repair page explains the part between the meter and your house.

What affects the cost of frozen pipe repair

A frozen but unbroken pipe may only need thawing. A split pipe needs a cut-out and replacement of the damaged section, and cost depends on access, material and how many splits. After-hours visits cost more. Water damage restoration is separate from the plumbing repair, and insurance often covers sudden bursts but not slow leaks.

A hard freeze hits Calumet City and Lansing at the same time, so expect busy phones. Our service areas page lists the towns our emergency line covers.

A pipe that has split from freezing is a repair covered by the Illinois Plumbing Code, and the Illinois Plumbing License Law says the person who repairs it must be licensed. Thawing guidance here is general. Follow the instructions that came with any heat tape or heater you use.
