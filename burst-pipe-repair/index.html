What to do in the first five minutes of a burst pipe

A burst pipe is a true emergency, and our plumbers answer the line 24/7, including overnight and on holidays. Same-day visits are normal for burst pipes, and we will tell you on the phone what is realistic for your address.

Find the main shutoff valve, usually where the water line enters the house near the meter, and turn it clockwise until it stops. Open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the lines. If water is near outlets, a ceiling fixture or the breaker panel, switch power off only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water, and otherwise stay out and call.

Why pipes burst in Calumet City winters

Water expands about nine percent as it freezes, and the ice plug does the damage. A pipe usually splits where the ice plug sits or just beyond it, so the leak often shows up after the thaw rather than during the freeze. Exterior walls, unheated garages, crawl spaces and pipes beside a basement window are the usual spots.

A burst that happens in a heated house still has a cause. Corroded galvanized pipe can give way at a threaded joint, high water pressure can fatigue old fittings, and a failed solder joint can let go years after the original work. We look for the reason, because repairing a single split while the rest of the line is fragile just moves the problem.

How a burst pipe repair works

1

Stop and assess

We confirm the water is off, drain the line and find the split, often by opening the wall or ceiling at a small, planned point.

2

Cut out the bad section

We remove the damaged length back to sound pipe so the new section is not joined to weak metal.

3

Repair with matching material

Copper gets a soldered or press-fit section, PEX gets crimped fittings, and galvanized steel is usually a good reason to talk about replacing the run.

4

Pressure test and restore

We bring the water back slowly, check every joint under pressure and show you the finished repair before we leave.

Repair or replace the run?

SituationUsual choiceWhy
Single split in newer copper or PEXCut out and repairThe rest of the line is sound, so a section fixes it
Split near a corroded joint on galvanized pipeReplace the affected runCorrosion elsewhere in the line will fail next
Several leaks in one wall or floorRepipe the sectionRepeated repairs cost more than new pipe
Pipe in an exterior wall that froze twiceReroute or insulateThe location is the problem, not the pipe

When more than a section is involved, our repiping team can plan a replacement that keeps the walls you want to keep. If you cannot find the break, a leak detection visit pinpoints it before anyone opens finished drywall.

Winter freeze and thaw: what Calumet City homes should check

Lake Michigan water arrives cold, and Calumet City draws all of its drinking water from the lake, supplied by Hammond and Chicago according to the city's 2024 water quality report. The incoming line is where a freeze often starts, so locate your main shutoff before January, not during a freeze.

After any hard freeze, check the lowest ceiling and basement walls for dampness, look at the water meter dial for movement when every tap is off, and listen for running water in the walls. A moving dial with all fixtures off means water is escaping somewhere. See our water meter and high-bill leak checks for how that test works.

Insurance and burst pipes

Standard homeowner policies commonly cover sudden pipe bursts but often exclude damage from slow leaks and sewer backup. Ask your agent which applies, and keep photographs and the repair invoice for the claim.
