Why snaking sometimes fails and jetting works

A drain cable cuts a small channel through a clog. The grease, scale or mineral film on the pipe wall stays behind, and the line narrows again until the next backup. Hydro jetting uses a hose with a nozzle that fires water jets forward and backward, so it cuts the blockage and rinses the wall as it moves.

That is why jetting is the usual answer for repeat clogs in kitchen lines, laundry lines and main drains. It is also the better tool for long runs of cast iron or clay pipe that have built up scale over decades. We use cable machines for single fixtures and jetters for lines that need a deeper clean.

Emergency jetting: when a backup cannot wait

A main line that has backed up into a basement, a restaurant kitchen line that has shut down service or a floor drain that will not stop rising is an emergency. Our plumbers answer 24/7, and we can offer same-day jetting when equipment and crew are free.

Until we arrive, stop using water in the building. Do not pour chemicals down the line, and keep people out of the area where sewage is rising. If a drain is releasing water onto electrical equipment, stay away from it and call.

How a jetting visit goes

1

Camera first

We run a camera to see the line, the type of pipe and what is in it. Jetting a cracked or offset pipe can make it worse, so we check.

2

Pick the nozzle

A penetrating nozzle opens the blockage. A rotating nozzle scours the wall. A root cutter handles roots.

3

Jet the line

We work from the cleanout toward the street, moving the hose in passes and flushing the debris out.

4

Camera again

We re-run the camera so you see a clean pipe, or we show you what is wrong and what it would take to fix it.

If the camera shows a break or a belly in the line, we move to repair options on our sewer line repair page. For routine upkeep in commercial buildings, jetting on a schedule is cheaper than an emergency visit. Our commercial plumbing team can discuss a maintenance plan.

Lake Michigan water, old lines and what builds up

The city's 2024 water quality report says Calumet City's water comes from Lake Michigan and meets state and federal standards, so mineral scale is not the usual story here. In many homes the buildup is grease, soap and the grit that settles in older cast iron and clay lines.

The report does not tell us what your pipe is made of, and neither does a guess. A camera does. If your house has a cast iron main with rough, flaking walls, jetting can restore flow, but we will show you the camera footage before and after so the decision is yours.
