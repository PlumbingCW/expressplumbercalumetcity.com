When a clogged drain is an emergency

A slow bathroom sink can wait until morning. Water rising in a tub when the toilet flushes, a floor drain that bubbles, or sewage smell near the lowest drain in the house cannot. Our emergency plumbers take these calls 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, and we can usually offer a same-day visit when you call.

While you wait, stop running water in the house, skip the dishwasher and washing machine, and hold off on flushing if more than one fixture is affected. Move rugs and boxes away from the floor drain. Please do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a standing backup, because it can splash on the plumber who has to work in that water.

How we clear a clog, from simple to serious

Most household clogs sit in the branch line behind a single fixture, and a cable machine handles them. We feed a cable through the trap or a cleanout, break through the blockage, and pull the debris back. Kitchen lines usually carry grease and food scrap. Bathroom lines carry hair and soap film. Laundry lines carry lint.

When a cable only punches a hole through the blockage, the clog tends to return within weeks. That is the point to consider hydro jetting, which scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water. If a line has clogged more than once, a sewer camera inspection shows whether a belly, a crack or tree roots are the real cause.

Signs the problem is the main line, not one drain

Lowest drain goes first

Water shows up in the basement floor drain or shower before anything else. That points to the main line, because the lowest opening is where the water finds the surface.

Toilets and tubs react to each other

Running the washer makes the toilet gurgle or the tub rise. Fixtures sharing one line are fighting for the same blocked section.

Backup after rain only

A line that drains fine in dry weather and backs up in storms may be overloaded downstream. We will check your side first and tell you plainly if it is not your pipe.

Smell without a visible clog

A dry trap, a failed vent or a cracked line can all smell like sewage. A short inspection sorts out which one it is before anyone cuts a hole.

Drain backups in Calumet City after heavy rain

The city of Calumet City reports that the Little Calumet River levee was built after the floods of 1981 and 1982, and local news coverage of the July 2023 flooding said officials blamed much of the damage on sewer backups rather than river overflow. If your basement drain backs up only during a storm, the cause may be outside your house, and no amount of cabling will change that.

The city's flood prevention page suggests floor drain plugs, standpipes, overhead sewers and sewer backup valves for homes that see repeat backups. Our plumbers can tell you whether the blockage is on your side of the property line and, if it is, clear it. If your line is open and the city sewer is surcharging, we will say so and talk through backflow options.

Snaking and cabling: when a cable is the right tool

A drain cable is a coiled steel line that turns as it goes in. It breaks through a plug of hair, grease or paper and pulls some of it out. For a single sink, tub or toilet line, it is often all that is needed, and it costs less than jetting.

A cable has limits. It can punch a hole through a thick blockage and leave the walls of the pipe coated, so the clog returns. If the same drain stops again within weeks, ask for a camera look or a jetting quote instead of a third cabling.

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom drains. Grease cools and sticks to the pipe wall, food scraps pile up in the trap, and a disposal can push waste further down the line. A slow sink that gurgles when the dishwasher runs is a warning sign.

Run hot water and dish soap first and clear the trap under the sink if you are comfortable doing it. Skip chemical cleaners in a sink that holds water. If the standing water does not move, or the other kitchen drain backs up too, call before it overflows.

Does insurance cover a drain backup?

Many homeowner policies exclude sewer or drain backup unless you add a rider, and the city's own flood page notes that these riders cost extra. Read your declarations page or ask your agent before a storm, not after.
