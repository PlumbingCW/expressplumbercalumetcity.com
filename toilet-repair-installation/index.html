When a toilet problem is an emergency

An overflowing toilet, water leaking from the base or a toilet that will not stop running can damage floors quickly. Our plumbers answer 24/7, including nights and weekends, and we can often come the same day. Turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise to stop the water, and use the main shutoff if the valve is stuck.

If the toilet overflowed with sewage, keep children and pets away, avoid touching the water and call us. If other fixtures are backing up too, the problem is not just the toilet. Our drain cleaning page covers main line backups.

Wax ring and flange: the leak at the base

A toilet sits on a wax ring that seals it to the floor flange. If the ring fails or the toilet rocks, water seeps out and soaks the subfloor. Water at the base is more serious than a tank leak, because it rots the floor underneath. We replace the ring, inspect the flange, repair it if needed and check the floor.

If the toilet has been leaking for a while, the subfloor may be soft. We will tell you what we see so you can decide about further repair. If you cannot find the source, our leak detection team can check other lines.

Repair or replace the toilet?

Repair makes sense

The toilet is newer, the porcelain is sound and the problem is a flapper, fill valve or seal.

Replace makes sense

The porcelain is cracked, the toilet flushes poorly or needs repeated repairs.

Upgrade for water use

Older toilets can use far more water per flush than current models. A new one can reduce water use.

Comfort height and layout

Taller bowls, elongated seats and different rough-in sizes are worth checking before buying.

Toilet problems and Calumet City water

If you see crusty deposits at the fill valve or rim jets, call us and we can clean or replace the parts.

A toilet that gurgles or bubbles when another fixture drains may point to a vent or drain issue, and a toilet that backs up during storms may point to the sewer. Our sewer line cleaning page covers main line backups.
