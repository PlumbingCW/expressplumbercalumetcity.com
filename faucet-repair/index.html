When a faucet problem is an emergency

A faucet that will not shut off, a valve that sprays when touched or a supply line that has burst under the sink can flood a kitchen or bath. Our plumbers answer 24/7, including nights and weekends, and we offer same-day visits. Close the angle stop under the sink first. If it will not turn, shut off the main water valve.

A stuck shutoff valve is a common reason people call after hours, because a fixture cannot be repaired while the water is still on. We carry replacement valves and can swap a failed stop while we are there.

Many faucet problems are inexpensive repairs. We replace the cartridge or seals and test the faucet. If the faucet is older and parts are hard to find, replacement may cost less than repair. You can also pick the faucet and we install it.

The valve behind the faucet

Under most sinks are two small angle stops, one for hot and one for cold. They are the shutoff valves for the fixture. Old stops can seize, leak at the stem or fail to close when you need them. Replacing them is a modest job that makes every future repair easier.

Test your shutoffs

Turn each valve gently once a year. A valve that is stiff or weeps is due for replacement.

Replace braided supply lines

Flexible lines can fail with age, and a burst line can flood a cabinet. We replace aging lines when we are there.

Know your main

If a fixture valve fails, the main valve is your backup. Find it today.

Check aerators

A clogged aerator lowers pressure and sprays. A quick clean often fixes it.

Faucets, Lake Michigan water and lead

Calumet City's 2024 water quality report says its lead 90th percentile was 17 parts per billion against a 15 ppb action level, and it points to lead in drinking water coming primarily from service lines and home plumbing. It tells residents to flush the tap for several minutes before drinking and to use a filter certified by an accredited body if they are concerned.

If your faucet is old, ask us what it is made of and what the supply lines are. Replacing an old faucet with a lead-free fixture and clean supply lines is part of the picture, and our filtration page explains what a filter can and cannot do.
