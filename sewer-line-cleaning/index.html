A blocked main line: why it is urgent

When the main sewer line is blocked, every drain in the house suffers, and the lowest one, usually a basement floor drain or shower, shows it first. Our plumbers take main line calls 24/7, including overnight and on weekends, and we can usually offer a same-day visit.

Stop using water, including toilets, the washer and the dishwasher. If water is rising, move belongings off the floor, keep family and pets away and call us. Do not open the cleanout cap under pressure without a bucket and boots, because water behind the cap can rush out.

Signs your main sewer line needs cleaning

More than one fixture is slow

A single slow sink is a branch problem. When the tub, toilet and sink all drain slowly, the main is the likely cause.

Gurgling in other fixtures

Flush the toilet and the shower drain gurgles. Air is being pushed through traps because the main is restricted.

Backups that return

A line that clears for a few weeks and clogs again has a source, such as grease, roots or a belly.

Sewer smell outdoors

A sour smell near the cleanout or in the yard can signal a break or blockage.

How we clean a main sewer line

We locate your cleanout, usually in a basement, crawl space or yard, and open it carefully. A large cable machine with a cutter head chews through grease, scale and light roots. For heavier buildup, we move to hydro jetting, which rinses the wall.

We finish with a camera if the blockage was serious or the line has had repeat problems. The camera confirms the pipe is open and shows whether a defect is behind the clog. If it is, you can plan a sewer line repair on your schedule rather than waiting for the next backup.

Sewer backups in a low-lying area

Calumet City sits near the Little Calumet River, and the city's flood page reports six major river floods since 1981 and a levee built after the 1981 and 1982 floods. Even when a line is clean, heavy rain can push water into a home through a floor drain.

A backwater valve on the sewer lateral lets water out and keeps it from coming back in. The city's page suggests sewer backup valves and overhead sewers for repeat-backup homes. We can tell you whether your cleanout and floor drain are good candidates and what installation involves.
