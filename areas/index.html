Why local conditions matter

Communities along the Little Calumet River share flood and sewer concerns. The city of Calumet City says the river levee was built after the floods of 1981 and 1982, and reports in 2023 said the city and Lansing together received nearly $15 million from the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers to clear vegetation and rebuild it. If your basement floods in storms, tell us when you call.
