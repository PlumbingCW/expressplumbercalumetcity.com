Gold Coast in Calumet City: what listing sources say

Real estate listing sources describe Gold Coast as a single-family community in Calumet City, Cook County. One listing source places it south of River Oaks Road and west of Burnham Avenue. It lists homes built between 1949 and 1964, from 749 to 2,093 square feet, and notes a community pool and park.

Gold Coast is a subdivision name used in listings and not an official city district. Calumet City does not publish a neighborhood map that we could find, so we treat the listing boundary as a guide and not a survey. If you are unsure whether your street counts, the address you give us on the phone is what matters.

General Calumet City facts

The city's 2024 water department report says all drinking water comes from Lake Michigan, with 80 percent supplied by Hammond, Indiana and 20 percent by Chicago. This is a citywide fact and not a finding about this subdivision.
