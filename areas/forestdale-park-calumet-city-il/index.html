Forestdale Park in Calumet City: what listing sources say

A real estate listing source describes Forestdale Park as a single-family subdivision west of State Line Road and north of Michigan City Road. It lists homes built between 1910 and 1967, from 1,120 to 3,500 square feet, and mentions curbs, sidewalks, street lights and a park pond. Recent sales show addresses on Forestdale Parkway and Forestdale Park.

The listing's build years span more than fifty years, so houses here can differ a lot from one lot to the next. That is a reason to look at each house on its own and not to assume anything about its pipes. We found no official city map of the subdivision.
