No heat is an emergency in winter

When a boiler fails in January, pipes can freeze in a day or two. Our plumbers answer 24/7, including overnight and on holidays, and we can often come the same day. Check that the thermostat is calling for heat, that the boiler has power and that the pressure gauge is in the normal range.

If you smell gas or see soot around the boiler, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first. If you see water leaking from the boiler, shut off the cold fill valve and call us.

Steam and hot water systems

Steam boilers make steam that rises through pipes to cast iron radiators, and the condensate returns by gravity. They depend on correct water level, working traps and vents. Hot water boilers circulate hot water with a pump, and they need the right pressure and air-free lines.

Both types are common in older homes, and both are repairable. We service circulators, zone valves, relief valves, low water cutoffs, igniters and expansion tanks. If the boiler is at the end of its life, we discuss replacement options, and if your hot water comes from the boiler, our water heater repair page may help.

Repair or replace?

Repair makes sense

The boiler is sound and the failure is a control, pump or valve.

Replace makes sense

The heat exchanger is cracked, the boiler is very old or repairs have piled up.

Check for safety

Cracked heat exchangers and failing flues are safety issues. We do not leave an unsafe boiler running.

Think about efficiency

A new boiler can use less fuel. We can talk through the economics.

Winter freezes and radiators

A boiler problem in the cold months can lead to frozen pipes, and our burst pipe repair page covers what to do if a pipe splits. If you leave the house for a few days, keep the heat set above freezing and know where your main water shutoff is.

Calumet City's water comes from Lake Michigan and arrives cold in winter, according to the city's water quality report describing its source. A boiler uses little make-up water, but a system that needs constant refilling has a leak, and we can find it.
