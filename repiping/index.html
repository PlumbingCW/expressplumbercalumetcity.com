What repiping means and when it is time

Repiping replaces the water supply lines inside a house, the pipes that carry water to sinks, tubs, toilets and appliances. It is not the sewer or drain. The goal is to replace pipe that has corroded, restricts flow or leaks repeatedly with new copper or PEX.

Many homes need only a section replaced, such as a bathroom group or a basement run. A whole-house repipe is worth considering when problems keep showing up in different places, because the rest of the line is at the same stage of life. If a single break is your problem, see our burst pipe repair page.

Signs your pipes are due for replacement

Rusty or brown water

Corroded galvanized steel releases rust into the water, mostly after the water sits.

Low water pressure

Scale builds on the inside of old steel pipe and narrows it until a shower becomes a trickle.

Repeated leaks

A pinhole in one place and another a month later suggests the pipe is failing along its length.

Discolored stains on walls or ceilings

Water behind a surface leaves brown marks that return after paint.

Material you know is a problem

Lead, galvanized steel and polybutylene all have a track record of trouble.

Identify your pipe: a quick guide

MaterialHow to recognize itConcern
Galvanized steelGray, threaded fittings, magnet sticksCorrodes from inside, restricts flow
LeadDull gray, soft, scratches with a key, magnet does not stickLead in drinking water
CopperOrange-brown or green, soldered jointsGenerally durable, pinhole leaks can occur
PolybutyleneGray or blue plastic with crimp fittingsKnown failure history
PEXRed, blue or white flexible tubingModern, flexible, resists freezing damage

If you see lead, treat it seriously and read the section below. If you see galvanized steel, plan a project before it leaks. If you have copper or PEX and a single leak, a repair is probably enough.

Lead plumbing and what the city says

Calumet City's 2024 water quality report says lead in drinking water comes primarily from service lines and home plumbing, and that the city cannot control the materials used in home plumbing. It lists a lead 90th percentile of 17 parts per billion against an action level of 15 ppb, and tells residents to flush taps and consider a certified filter.

Homes built decades ago may have lead service lines, lead solder or galvanized pipe, and the city's lead service line inventory on calumetcity.org may show what applies to your address. A repipe is the moment to replace unknown or old material. See our water service line page for the pipe from the street.

Insurance and repiping

Insurance typically covers sudden damage, not replacement of worn pipe. Repiping is a maintenance project. If a leak has damaged finishes, document it for a claim.
