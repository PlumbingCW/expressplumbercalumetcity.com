Sewer backup: what to do first

Sewage in a tub, shower or basement floor drain is an emergency. Our plumbers take sewer calls 24/7, including nights and weekends, and we offer same-day visits for backups. Stop running water in the house, keep kids and pets away from the affected area and do not use the toilets.

Raw sewage carries bacteria, so avoid touching it and wear boots and gloves if you have to walk through it. Do not try to push a pipe or cable through yourself if the water is at the surface. Call us, and we will help you work out whether the main is blocked or broken.

How we find where the sewer line failed

We start with a camera on a flexible cable, which shows a pipe's material, the location of the break and the cause of the problem. A locator on the camera head lets us mark the surface above the defect, so a repair crew knows exactly where to dig, or whether a trenchless method will reach it from a cleanout.

A camera inspection is useful even when a line is not backing up. If you are buying a house or have had repeat clogs, our sewer inspection page explains how the report works. If the line is only clogged and not damaged, sewer line cleaning may be all it needs.

Common sewer line defects

Root intrusion

Tiny roots enter at a joint and grow into a mass that blocks flow. Cutting them clears the line for a while, but repair stops them.

Cracked or broken pipe

Clay tile and cast iron can crack from age, ground movement or heavy loads above them.

Belly or sag

A low spot in the pipe holds water and solids, which cause repeat clogs. Only a repair removes it.

Offset or separated joint

Soil shifts and a joint opens. Dirt enters the pipe and roots follow.

Collapsed section

A fully collapsed pipe blocks the line and needs excavation or lining.

We give you the camera footage, mark the location and explain what each option involves. A line with a belly generally calls for replacement, because lining follows the dip. A line with a single offset may only need a spot repair. We do not recommend a full replacement when a smaller repair will last.

Sewer problems in a flood-prone community

The city of Calumet City says its flood prevention efforts include a levee on the Little Calumet River built after the 1981 and 1982 floods, and local news coverage of the July 2023 flooding reported officials attributing the worst damage to sewer backups. A damaged lateral lets groundwater and stormwater in, which can add to the load on the line during heavy rain.

If your lateral is cracked, sound repair reduces infiltration and helps prevent backups. It does not stop a surcharged city main from pushing water back toward your house. For that, ask us about a backwater valve, and ask the city whether it is offering cost-sharing for sewer improvements.

Insurance and sewer line repair

Most homeowner policies do not cover a worn or root-damaged sewer line. Some utilities and insurers sell sewer line service plans, so check what yours includes before you start.
