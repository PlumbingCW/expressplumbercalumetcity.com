Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we handle when you call us or visit this website.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we ask for your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem so that we can send a plumber. We use that information to arrange and carry out service and to follow up on your request.

This website

This website does not have forms, accounts or online payments. We do not ask you to type personal information into the site. Like most websites, the hosting service may keep standard server logs that include your IP address, browser type and the pages you request.

Maps and fonts

Some pages embed a Google Map. Loading a map may send information about your browser to Google, which handles that information under its own privacy policy. Fonts and images on this site are served from our own hosting.

How we use and share information

  • To dispatch a plumber and complete your service
  • To answer questions about a past visit
  • To improve how we answer calls
  • To comply with the law when required

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the plumber assigned to your job, with service providers that help us operate, or when the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us not to contact you again, and you can ask what information we have about your call. Contact us at contact@expressplumbercalumetcity.com or call (888) 910-8644.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of this page shows the last update.
